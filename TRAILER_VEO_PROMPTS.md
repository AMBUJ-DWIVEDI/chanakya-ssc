## VEO RETRY TIPS
- If a clip drifts off-style: add "static tripod shot" or reduce the action to ONE motion per prompt.
- If faces morph weirdly: add "consistent face, no morphing, natural human features".
- If it renders text anyway: add "absolutely no letters or numbers anywhere in frame".
- Generate 2–3 variations per shot and pick; an 8s clip costs nothing extra on AI Pro quota.
